Questions

Things shop owners usually ask

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain name is registered in your name, and you can take your website anywhere, at any time.

I already have a Facebook or Instagram page. Why do I need a website?

Social pages are great for photos, but customers searching on Google want your hours, menu, phone number and location fast. A website shows all of that in one place and links to your social pages too.

How long does it take?

It mostly depends on how quickly you can send your menu, photos and details. I'll give you a clear date before we start.

Can you update my existing website instead?

Yes. Tell me what's wrong or out of date, and I'll give you a free quote to fix or refresh it.

What if my menu or prices change later?

With monthly care (£15 a month) I make those changes for you. Without it, I can quote for one-off updates.

Do I have to pay everything upfront?

No. You pay 50% to start and the other 50% only when your website goes live.
